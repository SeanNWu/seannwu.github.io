I'm a Rhodes Scholar at the University of Oxford, advised by David Clifton, and I work with the Torr Vision Group. In Fall 2026 I begin a Ph.D. in Computer Science at Stanford. My research focuses on the reliability of LLMs and how to make them trustworthy in science and medicine.

Previously I studied CS and Math at Pepperdine, advised by Fabien Scalzo, and interned with James Zou at Stanford. I've been awarded the NSF GRFP, the Goldwater Scholarship, and more.
